Projects

Design and Development of Next Generation Automated Security Operations and Response Tool (ASPER)

The project aims to build an advanced automated security operations and response tool to identify and mitigate vulnerabilities against sophisticated cyber threats. The tool is capable of automatically hardening security and providing automated responses to ongoing attacks while keeping business impacts in mind.

Funding: Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology, Government of India

Industry Partner: Tata Elxsi Pvt. Ltd.

Status: Ongoing

Budget: Rs. 2.52 Cr

Role: Project Investigator (PI)
