Dr. Preetam Mukherjee

Assistant Professor & Chair, School of Computer Science & Engineering, Digital University Kerala

Background

Dr. Preetam Mukherjee is an Assistant Professor in the School of Computer Science and Engineering (SoCSE), Kerala University of Digital Sciences, Innovation and Technology (formerly IIITM-K), Trivandrum, Kerala, India. Prior to joining DUK, he was with the Division of Network and Systems Engineering (NSE), School of EECS, KTH Royal Institute of Technology, Stockholm, Sweden, where he worked as a Post-Doctoral Researcher.

He worked as a Sr. Software Engineer in IIT Kharagpur, India, and as a Sr. Research Engineer in the Centre for Distributed Computing in Jadavpur University, Kolkata, India. He received his Ph.D. and Master of Engineering from the Department of Computer Science & Engineering, Jadavpur University, in 2020 and 2011 respectively. His B.Tech degree is in Computer Science & Engineering from the West Bengal University of Technology, Kolkata, India.

Funded Projects

Design and Development of Next Generation Automated Security Operations and Response Tool (ASPER)

Funded by the Ministry of Electronics & Information Technology, Government of India (2025–2027), this project aims to build an advanced automated security operations and response tool to identify and mitigate vulnerabilities against sophisticated cyber threats.
