Background

Dr. Preetam Mukherjee

Assistant Professor, School of Computer Science & Engineering
Digital University Kerala
  • Assistant Professor at Digital University Kerala (formerly IIITM-K).
  • Post-Doctoral Researcher at KTH Royal Institute of Technology, Sweden.
  • Previously at IIT Kharagpur and Jadavpur University.
  • Ph.D. and M.Eng. from Jadavpur University.
  • Research interests include attack modeling, network hardening, incident response, 5G and Smart Grid security.
