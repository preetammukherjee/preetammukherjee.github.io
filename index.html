Background

Dr. Preetam Mukherjee is an Assistant Professor in the School of Computer Science and Engineering (SoCSE), Kerala University of Digital Sciences, Innovation and Technology (Digital University Kerala) formerly IIITM-K, Trivandrum, Kerala, India. Prior to joining DUK, he was with the Division of Network and Systems Engineering (NSE), School of EECS, KTH Royal Institute of Technology, Stockholm, Sweden, where he worked as a Post-Doctoral Researcher.

He worked as a Sr. Software Engineer in IIT Kharagpur, India, and as a Sr. Research Engineer in the Centre for Distributed Computing in Jadavpur University, Kolkata, India. He received his Ph.D. and Master of Engineering from the Department of Computer Science & Engineering, Jadavpur University. His B.Tech degree is in Computer Science & Engineering from the West Bengal University of Technology, Kolkata, India.
